Climb

Maximum rate is Figure 5-6 at 2,400 lb. Lighter weights are scaled by 2,400 divided by weight. That scale is an estimate. Time, fuel, and distance use the standard-temperature schedule, stretched by the day’s rate of climb.

Climb to cruise

Climb from the departure altitude to cruise, along the distance the day’s rate of climb will cover. The path flattens as the rate falls off.

Landing

Landing pattern

Left traffic. Upwind and crosswind are a climb at Vy for the arrival airport. Downwind, base, and final are one target inside the Section 4 landing speeds. Base is the leg where the flaps change on the way to final. Flaps of 10° stay under 110 KIAS. More than 10° stays under 85.
